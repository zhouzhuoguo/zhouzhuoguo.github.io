I received my B.S. in Communication Engineering from the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Beijing Jiaotong University (Beijing) in June 2025. I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Massachusetts Amherst (Fall 2026), where I will be advised by Prof. Jun Yao. My research interest is energy-harvesting devices and self-powered electronic systems for sustainable, low-power sensing. You can find more information through my CV.

Research Interests

⚡ Energy-Harvesting Devices & Self-Powered Systems

  • Harvesting clean energy from ubiquitous natural sources (e.g., ambient humidity and moisture).
  • Integration strategies that make harvested energy easy to use in electronic devices.

🔌 Circuit & System Integration for Sensing

  • Low-power circuit/system design and signal processing for self-powered sensing.
  • Wearable platforms for health monitoring (e.g., physiological signals).

🌱 Sustainable (Green) Electronic Materials

  • Renewable, environmentally friendly materials and device fabrication to reduce electronic waste.

Previous Research

🚗 Virtualized Platform-Based 5G Network Planning and Design (Graduation Project)

  • Leveraged the Lingte 5G virtual simulation platform and proposed an end-to-end network-planning framework for cross-regional connected-vehicle scenarios.

📡 Continuous Aperture MIMO for Electromagnetic Information Theory

  • Multi-user XL-MIMO: Uses a random-field model to derive MI expressions under multi-user interference and varying noise, quantifying the effects of discrete sampling and noise power on performance.
  • CAP-MIMO (non-paraxial): Derives tractable MI formulas via dyadic Green’s functions and shows how azimuth, sampling density, and receiver motion govern capacity and MI statistics.
